🌱 Clean and Green Technology
📖 Overview
Clean and Green Technology is a web-based waste management platform designed to promote environmental sustainability through active citizen participation and technology-driven solutions.

The platform enables citizens to report plastic and solid waste using live camera capture with automatic GPS location tagging. Reports are verified by municipal authorities, and users are rewarded with Green Coins for valid contributions. These coins can be redeemed for eco-friendly products through an integrated Eco Marketplace.

By combining waste reporting, rewards, environmental education, and gamification, the system creates a sustainable ecosystem that benefits citizens, authorities, and the environment.

🎯 Problem Statement
Improper disposal of plastic and solid waste has become a major environmental concern. Traditional waste management systems often face challenges such as:

Lack of real-time waste reporting
Limited citizen participation
Inadequate monitoring and tracking
Fake or duplicate complaints
Delayed response from authorities
The Clean and Green Technology platform addresses these issues through a modern, technology-driven solution.

🔄 Workflow

User captures waste image through live camera.
GPS location is automatically attached.
Report is submitted to the system.
Admin reviews the report.
Report is approved or rejected.
Cleanup task is assigned.
Green Coins are credited upon approval.
User redeems rewards via Eco Marketplace.
User participates in educational activities to earn additional rewards.
